## One-line vision

MAYA is an AI-powered **digital evidence investigation platform**. Deepfake detection is one analysis engine—not the whole product.

## Phase 2 implication

Evidence data engineering prepares a reproducible, memory-efficient training corpus for the AI Analysis Layer without mutating original evidence/dataset bytes and without implementing models, Flask routes, or UI.
